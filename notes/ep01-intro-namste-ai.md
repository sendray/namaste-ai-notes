## 📖 Course Philosophy  
Akshay Saini’s teaching style focuses on **depth, clarity, and fun**.  
He makes complex topics approachable, ensuring learners not only understand *how* things work but also *why*.  

## 📌 Pre‑requisites  
While AI spans multiple domains - Machine Learning, Advanced Python, Statistics, Linear Algebra, Mathematics, Neural Networks - this course is designed to be accessible.

All you need is:  
🔹 Basic programming knowledge  
🔹 Understanding of web applications  
🔹 Awareness of how software works  

## 📝 How to Get the Most Out of This Course  
🔹 **Make Your Own Notes (MYON):** Reinforce concepts in your own words.  
🔹 Complete assignments and practice independently.  
🔹 Engage with the **Namaste AI community**.  
🔹 Share your learning on social media to boost your profile and connect with peers.

## 💡 How to Learn from This Course

🔹 Make notes and complete assignments.

🔹 Stay curious - experiment, explore, and resolve doubts independently.
