## What is Artificial Intellignce?

> *“Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the science and engineering of building machines that can perform tasks requiring human-like intelligence - such as reasoning, learning, problem-solving, perception, and creativity.”* 🚀 

Machines that can:  
♟️ Play chess  
📧 Detect spam  
🎬 Recommend movies  
🚗 Drive a car  
✍️ Write poems  

👉 Yes, these are all **AI**.  

## 🔹 AI Journey Timeline  

🔹 **1950 – Alan Turing** ➡️ *“Can machines think?”* → The **Turing Test**  
🔹 **1955 – John McCarthy** ➡️ Term **Artificial Intelligence** coined  
🔹 **1950s – 1980s: Rule‑Based AI** ➡️ Human‑written rules, brittle systems  
🔹 **1986 – Synthetic Learning** ➡️ Early attempts beyond static rules  
🔹 **1997 – IBM Deep Blue** ➡️ Defeated Garry Kasparov in chess  
🔹 **1990s – Machine Learning Era** ➡️ Training data → Models learn → Predictions  
🔹 **2000s – Deep Learning** ➡️ Neural Networks + GPUs + Internet + Big Data  
🔹 **Computer Vision Revolution** ➡️ ImageNet dataset → Face unlock, self‑driving cars  
🔹 **2012 – AlexNet** ➡️ Breakthrough in deep neural networks  
🔹 **NLP Progress** ➡️ Bag of Words → N‑grams → RNNs → LSTMs  
🔹 **2017 – Transformers** ➡️ *“Attention is All You Need”* → Foundation for GPT  
🔹 **LLMs (Large Language Models)** ➡️ Trained on massive datasets, human‑like text  
🔹 **Generative AI (GenAI)** ➡️ From predictions → to creativity (text, images, stories)  
🔹 **2022 – ChatGPT Moment** ➡️ Conversational AI, RLHF, memory, multimodal  
🔹 **2025 – Agentic AI (Today)** ➡️ Think, plan, code, search, use tools, solve problems autonomously
